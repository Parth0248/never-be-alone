## Talking Points for Slide 3

**When presenting this architecture:**

1. **"Three simple layers"** - Input, Processing, Output
2. **"Multimodal at the core"** - Reka handles audio AND vision simultaneously
3. **"Intelligent orchestration"** - ASI:One coordinates multiple agents
4. **"Universal memory"** - Supermemory ensures nothing is forgotten
5. **"Real-world integration"** - Output connects to everything you use

**Key Message:**
> "This isn't just another AI assistant. It's a complete system that bridges your physical reality with intelligent digital action."
